Common Questions

Where does it pull the dates from? ▼
We can connect this to your existing fleet management software (if it has an open API like Tenstreet or Samsara), or we can build you a simple, secure Airtable database where your safety manager just drops in the driver's info and expiration dates.
Why the 6-month minimum lock-in? ▼
Federal telecommunications law now requires businesses to formally register their EINs with major carriers to send automated SMS. The 6-month term ensures we cover the upfront labor and filing fees required to prevent your safety texts from being blocked as spam.
Can drivers upload their new cards? ▼
Yes. The text messages include a secure upload link. When the driver takes a picture of their renewed Medical Card, it drops directly into your compliance folder so your safety manager can approve it.
