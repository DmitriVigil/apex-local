Common Questions

Why do you charge a Blueprint fee? ▼
Building custom software requires reviewing deep technical documentation. The blueprint fee covers our engineering time to evaluate your specific systems and guarantee the connection is actually possible before you commit to a major build.
What does the monthly retainer cover? ▼
It covers the dedicated cloud servers running your scripts, continuous error-log monitoring, and immediate debugging if one of your software providers (like Shopify or QuickBooks) updates their API protocols and breaks the connection.
Is my data secure? ▼
Yes. We use standard OAuth 2.0 or secure bearer tokens for authentication. Our middleware acts strictly as a "passthrough" pipeline. We process the payload in memory and forward it; we do not permanently store your client or financial data on our servers.
