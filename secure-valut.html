Common Questions

Is this secure and compliant? ▼
Yes. The web portal uses 256-bit TLS encryption. Files are never stored on our intermediate servers; the API pipeline passes the document directly from the client's browser into your encrypted Google Workspace or Microsoft OneDrive.
Why the 6-month minimum lock-in? ▼
Federal telecommunications law now requires businesses to formally register their EINs with major carriers (AT&T, Verizon) to send automated SMS. The 6-month term ensures we cover the upfront labor and filing fees required to prevent your texts from being flagged as spam.
Does the client need to download an app? ▼
No. It is a lightweight web application. They click the link in their text messages, select a file or take a picture with their phone camera, and click upload. Zero portal logins required.
Should I get this module, or the Bundle? ▼
If your biggest administrative bottleneck is collecting tax forms or IDs, start here. But if you also want to capture new leads while you are in court or meetings, and generate automated 5-star reviews, the Professional Firm Bundle gives you all three systems at a massive discount compared to buying them individually.
