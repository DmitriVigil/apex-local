Common Questions

Does the AP auditor pay the invoice? ▼
No. Our software operates strictly as a read-only auditor. It cross-references the data and outputs an "Approved" or "Flagged" status into your AP dashboard. A human still initiates the payment.
Why the 6-month minimum lock-in? ▼
Federal telecommunications law requires businesses to formally register their EINs with major carriers to send automated SMS. The 6-month term ensures we cover the upfront labor and filing fees required to prevent your safety texts from being blocked as spam.
Where does the DOT system pull dates from? ▼
We can connect this to your existing fleet management software (if it has an open API like Tenstreet or Samsara), or we can build you a simple, secure Airtable database where your safety manager manages the roster.
