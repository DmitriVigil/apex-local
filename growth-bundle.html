Tech & Setup Questions

Do I need to change CRMs? ▼
No. The automations run silently on top of tools like Jobber, ServiceTitan, and QuickBooks via secure webhooks. You change nothing about how you currently work.
Why the 6-month minimum? ▼
Federal law now requires businesses to register their EIN with carriers (AT&T/Verizon) for automated SMS. The 6-month term ensures we cover the filing costs and labor required to keep your texts from being blocked as spam.
