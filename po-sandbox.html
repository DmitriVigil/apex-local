Common Questions

Does this system pay the invoice for me? ▼
No. To ensure zero financial liability, our software operates strictly as a read-only auditor. It cross-references the data and outputs an "Approved" or "Flagged" status into your existing AP dashboard or a custom spreadsheet. A human still initiates the actual payment.
How does it read the PDFs? ▼
We utilize enterprise-grade OCR (Optical Character Recognition) APIs capable of extracting line items, quantities, and totals from structured and unstructured vendor invoices, even if the formats differ from vendor to vendor.
Should I get this module, or the Fleet Bundle? ▼
If your main pain point is back-office financial bleeding, start here. But if you are managing trucks and also struggling to keep track of CDLs, Medical Cards, and safety compliance, the Fleet Operations Bundle gives you both the AP Auditor and the DOT Safety Tracker at a heavily discounted rate.
