Common Questions

Why don't you have a flat rate? ▼
A single-truck plumber needs a very simple decision tree to book an estimate. A multi-location medical clinic requires complex compliance guardrails, deep logic branches, and heavy API integrations. We quote fairly based on the exact complexity of your specific workflows.
Will it make up prices? (Hallucinate) ▼
No. We use a strict engineering method called Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). The AI is hard-coded to only pull answers from the documents we feed it. If a customer asks a question that isn't in your knowledge base, the AI defaults to transferring the call to a human.
Does this replace my front desk employee? ▼
Absolutely not. Your front desk is highly valuable. This AI acts as their assistant. It catches the repetitive "What are your hours?" and "Do you have this part?" questions, filtering the noise so your staff can dedicate their time to high-value consultations and complex sales.
Does it work via text message or on my website? ▼
Both. We can embed the assistant on your website as a chat widget, or we can route it through a dedicated phone number so it handles incoming SMS messages and voice calls simultaneously.
