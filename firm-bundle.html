Common Questions

Is the document transfer compliant for legal/tax data? ▼
Yes. The web portal uses 256-bit TLS encryption. Files are never stored on our intermediate servers; the API pipeline passes the document directly from the client's browser into your encrypted Google Workspace, Microsoft OneDrive, or secure local server.
Does this integrate with my practice management software? ▼
We can route the missed-call lead data directly into the intake pipelines of most modern CRMs and practice management tools (like Clio, MyCase, or TaxDome) via secure webhooks.
Do my clients need to download an app? ▼
No. The entire system is built around SMS and lightweight web apps. Your clients simply tap the text message link on their phone to upload a tax form or leave a Google Review. Zero friction, zero logins.
Why is there a 6-month minimum commitment? ▼
Federal telecommunications law requires businesses to formally register their EINs with major carriers to send automated SMS. The 6-month term ensures we cover the upfront engineering and legal filing fees required to prevent your texts from being flagged as spam.
